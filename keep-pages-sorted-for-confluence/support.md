# Keep Pages Sorted Alphabetically for Confluence: support

Write to **support@brendenbushman.com** with your site's address, the space key, and what you expected and saw.

- We answer within 5 business days, and within 24 hours when Atlassian marks a request critical.
- Security reports go to the same address, with the subject "Security", and we treat them as critical.
- We never ask for your password, an API token, or access to your site: the app needs none of them, because it calls
  Confluence through Atlassian's Forge platform.

If a page keeps its place, look first at its edit restrictions: a page whose restrictions leave out Keep Pages Sorted is
not moved until the app is added to its editors.

What helps us most is the time it happened, to the minute and with your time zone, and what you did: created, renamed,
moved or dragged a page, or changed the setting.
The app logs one line per job, with only the kind of job and counts of what it did, one line for each change it drops
because its own move caused it or sorting is off in its space, and one line when a space admin saves the setting, never
page ids, titles or content, so the time is how
we find the right line.
