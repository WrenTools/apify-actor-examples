# Edit Inheritance for Confluence: support

Write to **support@brendenbushman.com** with your site's address, the space key, and what you expected and saw.

- We answer within 5 business days, and within 24 hours when Atlassian marks a request critical.
- Security reports: the same address, subject "Security". We treat them as critical.
- We never ask for your password, an API token, or access to your site.

What helps us most: the time it happened, to the minute, with your time zone, and what you did (created, moved, copied
or changed restrictions on a page). The app logs one line per job with only the kind of job and counts of what it did (and of Confluence's slow-down
answers),
and one line when a space admin turns it on or off, never page ids, names or content, so the time is how we find the
right line.
