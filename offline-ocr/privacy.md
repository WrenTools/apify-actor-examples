# Offline OCR privacy policy

*Offline OCR: Copy Text from Screen, a Chrome extension by Wrenkit. Effective 21 October 2026.*

**In short: the extension reads text on your own computer, keeps nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.**

## What the extension handles

When you click its button or press its shortcut, and only then, the extension takes a picture of the tab in front of
you so that it can read the text in the area you select. On its image page, it reads the image you paste, drop or
choose. That picture or image is the only data it handles.

- It is read in your computer's memory by the text recognition engine (Tesseract) built into the extension, and
  discarded when the reading is done.
- It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The extension contains no code that contacts a server; it works with your
  network switched off.
- Nothing is stored: not the picture, not the text, not a history. The extension has no account, no analytics and no
  tracking.
- The text is copied to your clipboard only when you press Copy.

## Permissions

- `activeTab`: lets the extension see the current tab only after you click its button or press its shortcut.
- `scripting`: shows the selection box and the result panel on that tab.
- `offscreen`: runs the text recognition inside the extension, in the background.

It has no access to any website until you use it, and none to your browsing history.

## Sharing and selling

We do not collect your data, so there is nothing to sell, share or transfer. The use of information received by this
extension adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this page will say so before that version is published, with a new
effective date.
