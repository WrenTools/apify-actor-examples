# Wren OCR privacy policy

*Wren Offline OCR: Copy Text from Screen, a Chrome extension by WrenTools. Effective 28 October 2026.*

**In short: the extension reads text on your own computer, remembers only your settings there, and sends nothing
anywhere.**

## What the extension handles

When you click its button, press its shortcut or choose "Copy text from this image" on a picture, and only then, the
extension takes a picture of the tab in front of you so that it can read the text in the area you select or in that
picture. On its image page, it reads the image you paste, drop or choose. Apart from your settings (below), that
picture or image is the only data it handles.

- It is read in your computer's memory by the text recognition engine (Tesseract) built into the extension, and
  discarded when the reading is done.
- It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The extension contains no code that contacts a server; it works with your
  network switched off.
- The picture, the text and the pages you read are never stored, and there is no history. The extension has no
  account, no analytics and no tracking.
- The text is copied to your clipboard only when you press Copy.

## Your settings

The extension remembers three settings, so that the next reading starts the way you left it: the language you last
picked, and whether "Join lines into sentences" and "One line" are on. They are kept on your computer in Chrome's
storage for the extension (not synced to your other browsers), never sent anywhere, and removed when you remove the
extension. Nothing else is kept.

## Permissions

- `activeTab`: lets the extension see the current tab only after you click its button, press its shortcut or choose
  "Copy text from this image".
- `scripting`: shows the selection box and the result panel on that tab.
- `offscreen`: runs the text recognition inside the extension, in the background.
- `contextMenus`: adds "Copy text from this image" to the menu Chrome shows when you right-click a picture.
- `storage`: keeps your settings, above, on your computer.

It has no access to any website until you use it, and none to your browsing history.

## Sharing and selling

We do not collect your data: your settings stay on your computer, so there is nothing to sell, share or transfer. The
use of information received by this extension adheres to the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/limited-use),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this page will say so before that version is published, with a new
effective date.
